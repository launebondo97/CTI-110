# The Robot Sandwich Assignment
## Assumptions
**Robot Constraints**: The robot executes commands chronologically, possesses no human intuition, and stops moving if a command is physically impossible.
**Initial State**: Refrigerator, pantry, cabinet, and silverware drawer are completely closed. All items inside are in standard containers.
1 Mayonnaise Jar
1 Package of scliced turkey
1 Loaf of sliced bread
1 butter knife
1 flat plate

## Numbered steps:
## Step 1: Preparation and Setup
**Locate the ingredients**:
1.Extend hand to the refrigerator door handle, pull to open.
2.Reach inside to locate the jar of mayonnaise and the package of sliced turkey. 
3.Grasp both items, remove them from the refrigerator, and place them **label side up** on the kitchen counter.
**Locate the bread**: 
1.Move to the pantry door, turn the handle, and pull open.
2.Locate the loaf of sliced bread. 
3.Grasp the bag and place it on the counter next to the ingredients.
**Locate the tools**:
1.Grasp the silverware drawer handle and pull outward to open.
2.Lower hand **into the drawer compartment**, grasp one butter knife by the handle, lift it out, and place it flat on the counter. 
3.Move to the cabinet door, pull open, retrieve one clean, flat plate, and place it next to the knife.
**Close all storage doors**: 
1.Push the refrigerator door until it latches shut.
2.Push the pantry door until it latches shut.
3.Push the silverware drawer until it is completely flush with the counter.
4.Push the cabinet door until it closes
## Step 2: Building the Base
**Prepare the bread bag**: Locate the plastic twist-tie or clip on the opening of the bread loaf package. Unwind or remove it and place it on the counter.
**Extract the bread**: Insert your hand into the open hole of the bread bag. Pinch fingers to grasp exactly two individual slices of bread. Pull them horizontally out of the bag.
**Position the bread**: Place the two slices of bread flat on the surface of the plate, lying side-by-side so they lay flat without overlapping.
**Seal the bread**: Twist the open plastic end of the bread bag three times. Pick up the twist-tie or clip from the counter and secure it back onto the twisted plastic.
## Step 3: Applying the Condiments
**Open the mayonnaise jar**:
 1.Place your left hand firmly on the glass base of the mayonnaise jar to stabilize it.
2.Place your right hand on the plastic lid. 
3.Rotate the lid **counter-clockwise** until it detaches completely from the jar. 
4.Set the lid face-up on the counter.
**Scoop the mayonnaise**:
1.Grasp the handle of the butter knife with your dominant hand. 
2.Insert the flat blade of the knife down into the opening of the mayonnaise jar. 
3.Submerge the blade into the mayonnaise, then lift the knife straight up out of the jar. 
4.**Rotate your wrist** so the flat side of the blade holding the mayonnaise faces straight up toward the ceiling to prevent dripping.
**Spread the mayonnaise**: 
1.Bring the knife to the first slice of bread on the plate. 
2.Invert the knife so the side with the mayonnaise touches the **top-facing flat surface** of the bread. 
3.Drag the blade back and forth across the flat surface until the mayonnaise transfers from the knife to cover the bread evenly.
**Clean the knife**:
1.Drag the flat side of the knife blade **exactly once** across the top-facing flat surface of the *second* slice of bread to transfer any remaining residue. 
2.Place the knife down flat on the counter surface.
**Close the mayonnaise jar**: Pick up the jar lid. Place it flat over the jar opening. Rotate the lid **clockwise** until it meets firm physical resistance.
## Step 4: Adding the Filling
**Open the turkey package**: Locate the plastic pull-tab on the corner of the face-up turkey package. Grasp the tab and pull backward away from the container to peel open the plastic seal halfway.
**Extract the turkey**: Insert your fingers into the open cavity of the package. Pinch exactly two individual slices of turkey meat and lift them vertically out of the package.
**Place the turkey**: Lower the two slices of turkey flat onto the first slice of bread, placing them directly on top of the layer of mayonnaise. Adjust so the turkey does not hang off the perimeter of the bread slice.
**Close the turkey package**: Press the top plastic flap back down firmly against the rim of the bottom container to reseal the package.
## Step 5: Assembly and Finalization
**Assemble the sandwich**:
1.Use both hands to pick up the second slice of bread by its left and right outer edges. 
2.Lift it up 6 inches. 
3.**Flip the slice 180 degrees** along its horizontal axis so the flat side containing the knife residue now faces downward toward the plate. 
4.Lower it directly on top of the turkey resting on the first slice of bread. 
5.Align the four corners of the top slice of bread with the four corners of the bottom slice.
**Clean the workspace**:
1.Pick up the jar of mayonnaise and the package of turkey. 
2.Open the refrigerator door, place them inside on a shelf, and close the door completely. 
3.Pick up the loaf of bread. 
4.Open the pantry door, place it inside, and close the door completely.
## A Done When:
1.Pick up the butter knife from the counter, carry it to the kitchen sink, and drop it into the basin. 
2.Return to the counter. Leave the completed sandwich on the plate undisturbed. 
3.Terminate all operations.
